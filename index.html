DormRun.

Campus food, run to your dorm.

DormRun is a student-to-student delivery service at UMass Amherst. You order your food, and a fellow student runner picks it up and brings it to your residence hall lobby.

How it works

  1. Order your food yourself. Place and pay for your order directly with the restaurant or campus dining location (for example, through UMass's GET mobile ordering).
  2. Request a runner in DormRun. Enter your order number, the pickup location, and your residence hall.
  3. A student runner picks it up and meets you at your residence hall lobby.
  4. Confirm delivery in the app. The runner gets paid the delivery fee and your tip.

Pricing

$[FEE] delivery fee per order + optional tip

DormRun sells a delivery service only. We do not sell food. You pay for your food directly to the restaurant or dining location. DormRun charges only the delivery fee and any tip you choose to add, paid by card or Apple Pay in the app. 100% of tips go to your runner.

Policies

Cancellations

  • You can cancel a delivery request for free any time before a runner accepts it.
  • After a runner has accepted but before pickup, you can cancel and receive a full refund of the delivery fee and tip.
  • After your order has been picked up, the delivery fee is non-refundable, since the runner has completed the work.

Refunds

  • If your delivery is not completed (for example, a runner cannot pick up your order or doesn't arrive), you'll receive a full refund of the delivery fee and tip.
  • If your delivery arrives very late or something goes wrong in transit, contact us within 48 hours and we'll review it for a full or partial refund of the delivery fee.
  • Issues with the food itself (wrong items, quality, missing items in a sealed order) are handled by the restaurant or dining location you ordered from, since DormRun does not sell food.
  • Approved refunds go back to your original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days.

Disputes

If you have a problem with a charge, please email us first at [EMAIL] with your order details. We'll respond within 2 business days and work to resolve it directly.

Terms

  • DormRun is currently available to UMass Amherst students only.
  • Deliveries are handed off at residence hall lobbies or main entrances. Runners do not enter residence halls they don't live in.
  • Runners are UMass Amherst students. Please be respectful. We may remove users or runners for misconduct.
  • Delivery times are estimates and are not guaranteed.
